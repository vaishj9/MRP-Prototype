Tutorial: how to use the prototype

Follow these 6 steps (about 3 minutes). Open the Member App in one tab and the Staff Portal in another so you can watch the numbers change. No password is needed anywhere.

Start here: choose a role

Try it step by step

1
Member

Join Riverside Fitness

Choose Member, click Join, fill the short form and press Join now. On the payment screen press Pay (the test card is already filled in).

You see Payment successful and a receipt number, and get a Member ID. A new row appears in the Members table and in the Payments table. To show a failed payment, type 4000 0000 0000 0002 and press Pay: the payment is declined and logged. You can also choose Pay at the front desk, then collect it as Front Desk (step 3).
2
Member

Book a class at any gym

Open Book a Class, pick a different gym from your home gym, choose a class and date, then press Book class.

This is the cross-location booking members told us was missing. If the class is full you join the waitlist, and you get the seat when someone cancels.
3
Front Desk

Check the member in

Go back to the start page, enter as Front Desk, open Front Desk, type the Member ID, press Find, then Check in. If the member chose to pay at the desk, press Collect payment first.

The visit is saved, the member's check-ins this month go up by one, and the booking turns to "Checked-in". This replaces the paper sign-in sheet.
4
Gym Manager

Watch the dashboard update

Enter as Gym Manager and open the Dashboard. Members, check-ins today, fill rate and the live activity list change straight away. Open Class Fill to see the emptiest classes and press Invite at-risk members.

One shared data source means every screen shows the same, current numbers.
5
Head Office

Keep members: retention outreach

Enter as Head Office and open Retention. Members with fewer than 4 check-ins a month are listed. Choose an action (for example a free guest pass) and press Reach out.

The contact is logged. The result changes to "Back on track" when the member checks in 4 times, or "Cancelled" if they leave. This is how we track the cancellation goal.
6
Head Office

Pay staff without errors

Open Payroll. Choose a staff member and enter hours. Pay is calculated for you. Try 500 hours (blocked), or the same person and month twice (blocked), or an unusual number such as 140 hours (warning). Then press Approve on the new record.

No one types the pay amount, impossible entries are stopped, and each record is approved before it is final. This is how we track the payroll error goal.

Dashboard 1 compared with Dashboard 2

Try it: enter as Head Office, open the Compare with Dashboard 1 tab to see Dataset 1 (real baseline) next to Dataset 2 (simulated after launch). On the Dashboard tab, choose Dataset 2: simulated after launch in the Data shown box to see every chart on Dataset 2. Dataset 2 was generated with the same generator as Dataset 1, using assumptions that reflect the solution. It shows the target, not a measured result: cancellations 27.3% to 13.5%, class fill 44.2% to 67.4%, payroll errors 11.7% to 5.6%.
